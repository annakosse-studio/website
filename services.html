Packages Services Portfolio Guidelines

Translation & Interpreting

Translation covers legal, business, financial, institutional, and professional documents, rendering their content accurately and consistently for the intended reader. The focus is on terminology, context, confidentiality, and the purpose of the document.

Interpreting supports multilingual communication in business, legal, and court-related settings, remotely or in person. This may include glossary preparation, familiarisation with the procedure, and attention to the communication context.

AI-supported Translation, LQA & Post-editing

AI-supported translation can make multilingual work faster, but speed alone is not enough. The output still needs human review to check terminology, consistency, context, confidentiality, and suitability for the intended purpose.

LQA and post-editing help teams review machine-translated, AI-assisted, or otherwise multilingual content before delivery or publication. The focus is on accuracy, terminology, style, omissions, formatting, and recurring quality patterns that may affect the final result.

In-house LQA Guide

In-house LQA Guide is a practical resource for teams using AI-assisted translation, machine translation, or multilingual content workflows. It helps organise quality review before content is delivered, published, or reused.

The guide covers terminology, accuracy, consistency, omissions, formatting, target-reader suitability, and recurring quality issues. It is intended for non-language teams as well as language professionals who need a clear review structure.
